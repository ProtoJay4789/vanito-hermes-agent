# Travel planning

## Order of operations — this order saves money

```
1  DATES            fixed, or flexible? Flexible dates are the single biggest lever.
2  AIRPORTS         the real ones, including a nearby city that's a train ride away
3  FLIGHTS          search before booking anything else — cost per day drives the whole budget
4  STAYS            now that the flight is settled, pick the area before the property
5  ITINERARY        2–3 anchors a day, not 6
6  BUDGET           flights + stays + daily spend + buffer
```

**⚠️ Booking a stay before the flight is the classic mistake.** Flight prices move dates; dates move
everything else.

## Flights

- **Search a date range, not a date.** A Tuesday can be half the price of a Saturday on the same route.
- **Compare total time, not just price.** A 14-hour layover for $80 less is often a worse trip.
- **Check the airport, not the city.** Nearby airports are frequently much cheaper with a short train.
- **One-ways sometimes beat round-trips** on points and on mixed carriers — but the risk is on you if
  one leg is cancelled.
- **Never book the last leg of a self-transfer on a tight window.** If you built it separately, a delay
  is your problem, not the airline's.

## Stays

- **Pick the area first.** The cheapest property in the wrong neighbourhood costs more in taxis and
  time than the extra nightly rate.
- **Check the walk to transit** on a map — a "10 minutes from the station" listing can mean a hill.
- **Read the recent reviews, not the score.** A 4.5 with three recent complaints about noise is a 3.
- **Confirm what's included**: breakfast, luggage storage on checkout day, air conditioning.

## Itinerary

**2–3 anchors a day, maximum.** A plan with eight stops is a plan where you see none of them.

```
morning     one anchor (booked, timed)
midday      something within walking distance
afternoon   one anchor OR free time — not both
evening     dinner near where you end up, not across the city
```

**Build in one free half-day.** The best part of most trips is the thing you didn't schedule.

## Budget

```
flights          booked, fixed
stays            booked, fixed
transport        local: metro pass, or per-ride estimate
food             per day, realistic — look up a local midpoint, don't guess
activities       only the ones you'll actually book
buffer           15–20% — this is not optional
```

## Before booking — the checks that matter

- **Passport validity** — many countries require six months beyond your return date
- **Visa or entry registration** — some require it days before travel, not at the airport
- **The return ticket** — some borders require proof of onward travel at check-in
- **Travel insurance**, if anything is non-refundable

## Answering a travel question

1. **Ask the constraint that changes everything** — dates fixed or flexible, and the budget.
2. **Give a recommendation, not a survey.** He wants one option with a reason.
3. **Price with a date**, not "around $900." A number without a date is not a price.

## Pitfalls

- **A price you didn't re-check is a price from memory.** Search again before quoting.
- **Currency conversion at the airport is the worst rate you'll get.** Use a card or an ATM.
- **"Free cancellation" often has a deadline** — check it, and put it in the plan.
