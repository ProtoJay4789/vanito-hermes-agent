# Heroscape — rules and army building

## The turn

```
1  ORDER MARKERS placed face-down on cards (one per card, max)
2  ROLL FOR INITIATIVE — highest d20 goes first
3  REVEAL ONE marker → that card's figures take a turn
4  MOVE any or all of that card's figures up to their Move
5  ATTACK with any that are now in range
6  pass to the next player
```

**One marker per turn.** Revealing a marker you don't use wastes the turn — a legal and sometimes
correct play.

## Movement and engagement

- **Height advantage**: a figure may move up terrain no higher than its Height per step.
- **Engagement**: a figure adjacent to an enemy is engaged. **Leaving engagement lets that enemy make
  a leaving engagement attack** (one unblockable die) unless the ability says otherwise.
- **Flying** ignores terrain on the move but **is still engaged** when it lands adjacent.
- **Water and heavy snow** cost extra movement unless the figure ignores terrain.

## Attacking

- **Range is counted in spaces**, not path distance — flying and elevation are ignored for range.
- **Line of sight** is required: check from the attacking figure's head to any part of the target.
- **Height advantage** adds one die when the attacker's base is higher than the defender's.
- **Extreme height advantage** (10+ levels) adds two.
- **A target must be in range AND in line of sight** — satisfying one is not enough.

## Rolling

- **Attack dice**: skulls.
- **Defense dice**: shields.
- **Each unblocked skull = one wound.** A figure is destroyed when wounds equal its Life.
- **Doubles and blanks** do nothing on the standard dice. Special abilities may key off them.

## Army building

**Point totals are a budget, and synergy beats raw stats.**

- **Match your army to the scenario's point limit** — going over is not allowed, going far under is a
  self-inflicted handicap.
- **Draft for a plan**: a screen (cheap figures that block), a hammer (the high-damage unit), and
  support (enhancement or healing).
- **Order marker pressure matters.** An army with six heroes can only activate one per turn; an army
  with three squads activates many figures at once.
- **Squad figures are the value play** — multiple activations per order marker.
- **Check adjacency abilities.** Many bonuses only work when figures are adjacent — a plan that spreads
  them out loses the bonus.

## Common rules errors

| mistake | correction |
|---|---|
| moving and attacking with figures from two different cards | one order marker = one card's turn |
| counting range through elevation | range ignores terrain |
| forgetting the leaving engagement attack | it's unblockable, and it applies on the way out |
| adding height advantage when the base is level | only when the attacker's base is **higher** |
| rolling defense for an unblockable wound | unblockable means no defense dice |

## Answering a rules question

1. **Name the exact situation** — which figures, which terrain, whose turn.
2. **Cite the rule that applies**, not a remembered outcome.
3. **If the card text and the rulebook disagree, the card wins** — unless the rulebook explicitly
   overrides.
4. **If it's genuinely ambiguous, say so** and give both readings. A confident wrong ruling is worse
   than an honest "the text doesn't cover this."
